Reading sample

A customer with an unpaid invoice gets a reminder on day 7 and an overdue notice on day 14. A daily job at 09:00 UTC reads the invoices that are due, checks each status again just before it sends, and writes one row to reminder_log for every email. It covers RF1 to RF4, and it leaves the late fee to a later spec. An emphasised phrase has no italic font and is slanted by the browser; a strong phrase uses weight 600.

Characters the page needs: → ← ↑ ↓ − ≤ ≥ ≠ · § “quotes” – — … and in code ├── src └── tests │ ─ with digits 0O 1lI.
