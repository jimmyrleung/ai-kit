Invoice reminders: daily job and overdue notice

techspec.mdFeature integrationConfidence 92%11 files: 4 new, 7 changed3 illustrations

Amendment, 2026-11-12. The retry for bounced emails moved from the job to the mailer. Sections written before that date keep their original scope; see Open decisions.

Summary

A customer with an unpaid invoice gets a reminder on day 7 and an overdue notice on day 14. A daily job at 09:00 UTC reads the invoices that are due, checks each status again just before it sends, and writes one row to reminder_log for every email. It covers RF1 to RF4 and leaves the late fee to a later spec.

All work is in the billing service. There is no change to the public API. The blast radius is the invoice store, one new job and one new table; the mailer gains a retry that other callers do not use yet.

  • Reminder. Sent once, on day 7, in the customer's locale.
  • Overdue notice. Sent once, on day 14, and it names the late fee that applies from day 15.

Code baseline: billing at 4c1e9a2. Paths in this document start at src/reminders/.

Invoice lifecycle

Paid and Void are final. An invoice can be voided from Draft or Sent, and the job never touches an invoice in a final state.
